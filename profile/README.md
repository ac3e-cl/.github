## About us

AC3E is a Chilean research center of excellence hosted at **Universidad Técnica
Federico Santa María (UTFSM)** in **Valparaíso, Chile**. We conduct
world-class research in electrical and electronic engineering, bridging
fundamental science and real-world impact.

Our work spans, among others:

- Power and energy systems
- Signal and image processing
- Control and automation
- Communications and networks
- Biomedical engineering
- Embedded and intelligent systems

## What you'll find here

This GitHub organization hosts open and internal projects developed by AC3E
researchers, students, and collaborators: research software, datasets,
reproducible experiments, and engineering tools.
